# The Smith Wedding

A public demo of a wedding website. **James, Sophie, their friends, the estate and the village are all made up.**

Open it at https://demo.wentzel.wedding and type the invitation code `DEMO`.

- The envelope opens, and the whole invitation is there: the weekend, the venue, where to stay, what to wear, the
  wedding party, the photos and the questions.
- **What to wear** is shown by twelve 3D guests who breathe, look around and change pose. You can turn them and try
  the wedding colours on them.
- **Kindly Reply** takes any name you type and opens "your" invitation: who is invited, to what, when and where,
  then the reply itself (golf or the spa, the dinner, a song) and a thank-you with your own weekend.

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. There is no server behind this demo: a small script in the page answers the
page's own requests, and a reply lives in your browser tab only.

The real website this is a copy of is private: it holds a real guest list. This copy looks and works the same, with
every fact changed: the names, the dates, the estate (its map here is a drawn plan of a place that does not exist),
the places to stay (their telephone numbers are in a range kept for fiction, and the buttons say what they would do)
and the couple's dashboard, which is left out.

## Who made it

Jean Wentzel, for his own wedding. Under the invitation, at the foot of the demo, there is a band "About the
developer" with a form to write to him about a website of your own.
