The Venue

Getting there

Highland Meadow Estate
1 Meadow Lane, Mistvale

Directions to the main gate

We ask for your location once, only to work out the drive. Nothing is kept.

The drive

  1. From the city

    About 260 km and roughly 3 hours: the motorway east to Ashbridge, then the hill road north for about 30 km to Mistvale.

  2. From the coast

    About 190 km and roughly 2.5 hours: the valley road inland to Ashbridge, then the same hill road north to Mistvale.

  3. The whole drive
    From the airport
    From the city airport it is about 240 km, roughly 2 h 40 min, on the same motorway.
    The last stretch
    The paved way to the estate runs through Mistvale village: Heron Street, over the stone bridge, then Meadow Lane for about 2.5 km. Take it slowly: the sheep have right of way.
    The long weekend
    It is a long weekend, and the motorway is at its busiest on Thursday afternoon and Friday morning. Leave early if you can.
    Tolls
    There is one toll plaza on the motorway, just before Ashbridge.
    Fuel
    There are fuel stations in Ashbridge and in Mistvale.
  4. The main gate

    It is paved all the way to the gate. In Mistvale, pass the village green, turn left into Heron Street and cross the stone bridge. At the end turn right onto Meadow Lane: the gate is on your right after about 2.5 km, between two brick pillars.
